01 — Brand Positioning

Where We
Operate

01

The Audience

Urban professionals, 22–40, London. High-output people who treat their body as infrastructure — and their lunch break as the hinge between morning and afternoon performance.

02

The Product

Premium poke bowls. Above-average price point, justified by quality, freshness, and a bold visual identity. Fast but not fast-food.

03

The Space

Premium fast-casual. Competing not just with poke chains but with any brand that touches London's wellness, performance, and lifestyle culture.

Key Insights

What we know
about our people

01 Energy is the real product +
People don't come for food. They come to feel sharp, light, and back in motion. The bowl is the delivery mechanism for a feeling.
02 The lunch hour is the pivot point +
For white-collar London, 1PM is the hinge of the day. What you eat there either fuels or drags the afternoon.
03 Premium ≠ slow or joyless +
This audience rejects trade-offs. They want quality, speed, and pleasure — simultaneously. Honi actually delivers that.
04 Stories move people, facts don't +
A vivid bowl is forgotten. A bowl that belongs to a story — a pitch, a first date, a run, a reunion — lives in memory and gets shared.
The Positioning Idea
Charged.

Honi Poke is the fuel for people who don't stop — and the place where their stories happen. Every great day has a Honi in it somewhere.

Key Messages

Three ideas
we say loud

01 Fuel that keeps up with you +
For the runner, the founder, the creative director eating at their desk. Honi Poke matches your pace — told through real people, real moments.
02 The 1PM edge +
Every lunch break is a scene in someone's bigger story. The people who perform best in the afternoon made a better choice at lunch.
03 Colour is a promise +
What looks energetic, is energetic. The bold aesthetic of Honi Poke isn't decoration — it signals freshness, intensity, and life.

Why Now · Trend Context

Three forces
aligning

01 Swicy is the flavour of 2026 +
Sweet-heat is the #1 flavour trend of 2026, named by National Geographic, Tastewise and FlavourTrends as the defining combination of the year. McDonald's, Wingstop, Hershey and Takis all made structural commitments to swicy in Q1 — this is no longer a trend, it's an industry framework.
02 Protein culture is mainstream +
Post-pandemic wellness has made protein consciousness universal, not niche. Our audience expects it. More protein options make the choice easier.
03 The premium salad gap +
London lacks a fast-casual brand owning the premium salad space with real flavour credibility. Poke Salads fills that gap with Honi's existing DNA.
